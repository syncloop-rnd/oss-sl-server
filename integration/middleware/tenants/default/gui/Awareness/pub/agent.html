The square root of 2 (√2) is an irrational number, approximately equal
to 1.4142135623730950488016887242097....

It cannot be expressed as a simple fraction, and its decimal representation continues infinitely without repeating.

Interesting Facts:
  • √2 was the first known irrational number, discovered by the ancient Greeks.
  • It represents the length of the diagonal of a square with sides of length 1 (by the Pythagorean theorem).
  • It is also called Pythagoras' constant.
